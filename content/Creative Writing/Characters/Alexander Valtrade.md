### Basic Information

- **Name**: Name, aliases, AKAs
- **Age**: Or birthday if relevant
- **Occupation**: Current job and role in the society.
- **Role in Story**: Main character, antagonist, support character, etc.

### Appearance

- **Physical Description**: Height, build, eye color, hair color, and distinctive marks.
- **Style of Dress**: Usual attire, including any characteristic outfits.
- **Demeanor**: How they generally present themselves (confident, shy, aggressive).

### Personality

- **Traits**: List of key personality traits.
- **Values and Beliefs**: Core values or beliefs that motivate their actions.
- **Fears and Flaws**: Key vulnerabilities or weaknesses.

### Abilities and Skills

- **Talents**: Specific skills or competencies.
- **Challenges**: Any limitations or areas of struggle.

### Background

- **Family and Early Life**: Overview of family background and upbringing.
- **Education and Training**: Formal or informal education and training.
- **Key Life Events**: Major events that have significantly influenced their development.

### Motivations and Goals

- **Immediate Goals**: What they are currently striving to achieve.
- **Life Ambitions**: Long-term dreams and aspirations.

### Relationships

- **Significant Relationships**: Key friendships, rivalries, or familial ties.
- **Network**: Their social or professional circle and its impact on their life.

### Role in the Plot

- **Main Conflicts**: Conflicts they are involved in and their stakes.
- **Growth and Change**: How they evolve throughout the story.

### Miscellaneous

- **Secrets**: Hidden aspects that can drive the plot forward.
- **Quirks**: Unique habits or behaviors that make them memorable.
