## Hemlock the Witch
Colburn. 

A caster that was burned at the stake. A former Wing Commander of the Falconyx, skilled with a spear and well read and intelligent. Master strategist and skilled magic user.

Rescued and cared for after his burning. His right mouth and cheekbones are burned red, with a threaded scar that travels down his throat and across his chest and down his legs. 

Lore constantly speaks to Hemlock after having played violin to him in tribute. He will grant Hemlock powers of time reversal if he will get him memory or a body.

## Scarlet L'eilorian
A vampire with a blood-hydraulic brass left arm. Her skilled engineering and mechanical capabilities allowed her to craft a prosthetic that virtually replaced her arm. A princess of an old family, 

Hemlock was able to provide healing during integration and heart-ignition, as well as decorate the prosthetic.

She found him after his burning and nursed him back to a level of health that created a close bond. 

Her vampirism weakens her in the sun and she feeds in order to combat the sickness from overtaking her body. 

## Alexander Valtrade
Long dark hair and a face full of angles and avian features. Strong commander of the Thunderlight. 

## Starsinger
Werewolf with white hair and vibrant green eyes. Called so because she is the designated Speaker to the Moon. 

## Darellian Hawk
Captain of the Falconyx. Lean, muscular, and stout in structure. Wields a sword longer than expected, whose darkblade is seven swords, an anomaly. Alliance is with Hemlock after the burning that occurred.

## Carkech Malleus
Executioner for the city. Intimidating presence, foreboding gaze, and a deep gravelly voice with jet black hair and a thick beard. Arms powerful enough to wield a double-headed axe freely in combat. 

## Nicholas Geddan
Lab director of Novus Laboratories that was in charge of the energy division. Used clones of his son to infuse with a Fire Archelemental, but they constantly died. This caused him to use his actual son as the core experiment for AEVII, a human battery. 

## Lore
A dark cloaked figure that speaks from the moon. Darkly connected to Hemlock. Has the power to turn back time. Will grant seconds of time for capabilities.
