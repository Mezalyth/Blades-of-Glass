## Stylos Blade
Mightiest is the pen that is also the sword. It was a blade forged of night, glinting with stars, and said to have the power to rewrite history. To behold the weapon was to stare into a void in reality, as though it were not an object to be held, but a sword-shaped portal, galaxies and nebula shifting as your eyes played over its surface. One didn’t look at it, but through it, the edges of the thing blurred and vibrated as though it fought to contain itself.

The sword takes on whatever shape best suits the wielder, and tears into reality rather than cuts like a knife. It also has the ability to tear wormholes open and teleport the user across space. Potentially across time.
