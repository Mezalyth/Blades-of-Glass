## The Lands - Teilannis
Teillanis is the name of the world these characters inhabit. Full of various biomes, factions, and political ideologies. 

A natural land where magic and science once warred, there are ancient artifacts of superweapons of destructive power buried throughout Teilannis that give evidence of the Theory War, a decades-spanning conflict that culminated in science and magic being brought to grand heights. 

Archelementals stepped in and destroyed much of the population as arsenals of starbombs were dropped in vengeance. Cities were floated into the air to escape the onslaught from captured air elementals and provided a momentary surrender and peace as time passed hundreds of years ago.
