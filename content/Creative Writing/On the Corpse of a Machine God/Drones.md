[[I, the Machine God]] could not be everywhere, and so it made drones to observe, communicate, and destroy. While human labour was economically cheaper to its nuclear power reserves and less taxing on its processing power, humans have long been known for their desire to fight back. Their inability to listen.

Several classifications of drones exist, mostly for enforcement of rule, but there were also a few that were responsible for data collection.
