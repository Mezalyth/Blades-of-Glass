A focus device that allows the [[Terminal Knights]] to easily control and wield their Solar Flares. Built into a circlet worn across the brow, it syncs with the mental signals of the wielder and interprets their desires as fine-tuned controls. 

Lanterns are ancient tech modified and approved for use by the [[Fyrwyl]].
