Nuresh, the sun goddess, sent down a solar flare to destroy [[I, the Machine God]] and saved humanity. 

After freeing them from the chains of their misery, the sun became a deified thing, personified by Nuresh. In opposition to I, she rules over the following domains:

- Light
- Warmth
- Radiation
- Freedom
- Power
- Hope

A pantheon of gods follow her path, helping to guide humanity away from the artificial mind.

- Geshi - The god of agility, mobility, and skyward gaze
- Oppeith - The goddess of anger, rage, and blood
- Prauss - The goddess of clarity of mind, logic, and peace
- Khell - The goddess of endings, finality, and death
- Ura - The goddess of nature, earth, and growth
- Bareth - The goddess of sleep, dreaming, and wilderness
