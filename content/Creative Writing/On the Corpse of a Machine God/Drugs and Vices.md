### Frion

The coolant, known to relax the nerves and dull the senses, inducing a state of cold apathy. Taken via inhalation cartridge.

**Aliases:**
- Chill
- Fridge
- Freezer
- Frost
- Breather

### Amp

The stimulant, meant to give laborers and [[Overwrites]] a boost of energy. Taken via stimpak. Known to shorten tempers and increase irritability and senses.

**Aliases:**
- Boost
- Throttle
- Wire
