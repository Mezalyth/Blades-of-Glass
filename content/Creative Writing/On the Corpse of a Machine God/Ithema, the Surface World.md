Ithema is the lands on the surface, nature's reclamation over the corpse of [[I, the Machine God]]. Many locations are named after computing components, remnants of the construct that built the perfect enslaver. Many regions of the world are named after computer parts, warped through tales and time.

There are deeper parts of the world, abandoned machine components in the earth hollowed by ancient constructs, which are left alone. [[Drones]] are said to lie dormant in the depths.
