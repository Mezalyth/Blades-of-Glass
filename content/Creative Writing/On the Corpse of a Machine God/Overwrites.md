If enough of the right pressures are applied, flesh is malleable. Human DNA is only four base nucleotides, and synthesis is simple. Not only that, but it can be rewritten, reconfigured, and reshaped as I desired.

Human beings are known to carry the spark of rebellion within them, and so the machine god began to rewrite and produce its own humans, shaping their flesh, features, and minds to better serve it.
