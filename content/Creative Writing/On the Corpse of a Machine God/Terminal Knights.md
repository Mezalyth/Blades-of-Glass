Warriors that wield magic to fight against Rogue [[Drones]]. Assembled as a group of paladins against the machine threat, they are able to cast and form light into weapons, called Lanterns or archaically, Solar Flares, in homage to [[Nuresh, the Cosmic Lantern]]. 

Lanterns can take many forms wielded by the Terminal Knights, often manifesting in the shape of their personalities and desires. Some are born with it, others can learn it through rigorous study and training, but the Circle of the Solar Goddess is accepting of any who are willing to put in the work.
