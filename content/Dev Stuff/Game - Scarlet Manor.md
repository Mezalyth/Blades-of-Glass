## Premise
Scarlet the Vampire's manor is in disarray. Tend to in in a spooky resource management game. Power up your abilities, summon an army, and kill your sire.

## Game Modules

### Feeding
**Mechanic**
- More fed means better abilities / powerups (senses, etc.)
- Hide and seek
- Hidden threat plotline?

**Gameplay**
- Give marking poisons to people who want others killed
- Hide/seek? 


### Gardening
**Mechanic**
- Light Gardening, 8 plants per season
- Can mix into potions to sell potions at shops

**Gameplay**
- Seeds, plant, water, cultivate
- Ingredients to be sold or mixed into potions

### Organ Music
**Mechanic**
- Fun thing to do
- Unlocks future upgrades (first one is shoots a raven out of organ pipe)

**Gameplay**
- Musical notes quicktime rhythm
- Freeplay

### Potions and Poisons
**Mechanic**
- Mix ingredients
- Sells to market
- Can cause other

**Gameplay**
- Mix ingredients for stats
- 2 min, 3 max (fruit/leaf/herb/shroom?)

### Manor Upkeep
**Mechanic**
- Decorate manor
- Unlock other rooms
- Customize/build expansions/rooms

### Gargoyles
**Mechanic**
- Advice
- Humor bits
- Hint what to do next

**Gameplay**
- Chat/dialogue
- Two speak to you, one speaks in rock language.
- See/Hear/Speak no evil vibes

### Character Plots (Selling at Market)
**Mechanic**
- Stardew had 6 each
- 10 key characters with storylines, potions and poisons help move/eliminate/advance others

**Gameplay**
- Sell potions or poisons
- Refuse to sell potions or poisons
