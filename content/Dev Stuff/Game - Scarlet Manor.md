## Premise
Scarlet the Vampire's manor is in disarray. Tend to in in a spooky resource management game. Power up your abilities, summon an army, and kill your sire.

## Game Modules

### Feeding
**Mechanic**
- More fed means better abilities / powerups (senses, etc.)
- Hide and seek
- Hidden threat plotline?

### Gardening
**Mechanic**
- Light Gardening (100 plants, most poison)
- Can mix into potions to sell potions at shops

**Gameplay**
- Seeds, plant, water, cultivate
- Ingredients to be sold or mixed into potions
