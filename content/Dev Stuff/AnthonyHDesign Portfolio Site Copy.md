## About
### The Process
- **Discovery**
	- Meet the client
	- See what they care about
	- What counts as a win for them?
	- What's the heart of the message?
	- Solve the problem
-  **Research**
	- Find out what others are doing
	- Note the common themes and elements
	- What rules can we break?
- **Iteration**
	- Build the design
	- Does it solve the problem?
	- Does it communicate effectively?
