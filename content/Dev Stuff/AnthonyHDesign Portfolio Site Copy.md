### National Alliance for Public Charter Schools Booth
**The Challenge:** Design an engaging and accessible interactive experience for the 2023 Charter School Conference in Houston. The experience needed to:

- Create a welcoming and inclusive space for conference attendees.
- Effectively communicate and amplify the impact of the charter school mission through authentic user stories.
- Facilitate meaningful interactions and knowledge sharing among attendees.
- Adhere to strict building codes and budgetary constraints.

**Discovery & Definition:** The project began with a collaborative discovery phase, involving key stakeholders from the National Alliance for Public Charter Schools (NAPCS), including board members, the account executive, and the production manager. We also partnered closely with the booth construction vendor to ensure feasibility. Through these discussions, we defined the project's core objectives: to create an engaging and informative experience within a 20x40 ft. footprint, while adhering to a strict budget.

**Key User & Business Goals:**
- **Attendee Engagement:** Provide a comfortable seating area to encourage interaction and networking.
- **Brand Visibility:** Create a visually striking and memorable booth that prominently features the NAPCS brand.
- **Interactive Data Collection:** Design an interactive polling station to gather attendee opinions on key issues.
- **Storytelling & Sharing:** Facilitate a "share your voice" station for attendees to contribute their personal stories.
- **Practicality:** Integrate storage solutions into the booth design.

**Design & Implementation:** Leveraging the prime location at the conference entrance, we prioritized a visually impactful design. A multi-screen video wall, powered by a Mac Studio Pro, served as a focal point, displaying a compelling narrative of the NAPCS mission. This immersive experience was designed to capture attention and effectively communicate the organization's message.

To foster interaction, we developed a "share your voice" station featuring a unique ping pong ball polling system. This allowed attendees to participate in real-time data collection on controversial topics, creating a dynamic and engaging experience.

Visual design choices, including vibrant colors, a suspended banner, and a distinctive archway, were strategically employed to attract attention and create a memorable brand presence.

**Impact & Future Collaboration:** The NAPCS organization expressed exceptional satisfaction with the project, recognizing our deep understanding of their brand, our innovative technological approach, and the overall impact of the installation. This success led to them awarding our agency the contract to fully rebrand the conference for the following year, including the development of a templated system for future events.

### Grand Canyon Reelunion
**The Challenge**

The Grand Canyon Visitor Center sought to generate excitement for the reunion of the original IMAX film crew behind _Grand Canyon: The Hidden Secrets_. This milestone marked the sunsetting of the legendary 70mm film, requiring an experience that honored its legacy while driving engagement from media, family, and friends. The challenge was to design an immersive and interactive campaign that:

- Captured the grandeur and significance of the film’s impact.
    
- Created a high-touch, visually striking invitation experience.
    
- Reinforced the Grand Canyon Visitor Center’s brand and legacy.
    
- Integrated digital and physical touchpoints to drive visitor engagement.
    

### **Discovery & Definition**

Through close collaboration with stakeholders—including the Grand Canyon Visitor Center team and hospitality partners—we defined the core objectives:

- **Honoring the Film’s Legacy** – Incorporate design elements that reflected the film’s history and the natural beauty of the Grand Canyon.
    
- **Tactile & Digital Fusion** – Create a print invitation experience that felt as grand as the canyon itself while extending the reach via digital platforms.
    
- **Maximizing Engagement** – Ensure the campaign drove both in-person attendance and long-term brand engagement.
    

### **Key Experience & Business Goals**

- **Immersive Invitation Design** – Developed a unique, unfolding print piece housed in a custom film tin, evoking the 70mm film experience and prompting recipients to engage.
    
- **Brand Continuity** – Tied in deep red and golden hues inspired by the canyon, paired with typography and visual motifs from the original film.
    
- **Seamless Visitor Journey** – Designed a dedicated landing page and digital ad campaign directing users to event details, ticketing, and hospitality partners.
    
- **On-Site Brand Experience** – Updated signage and wall murals to reinforce the film’s legacy while promoting its successor, _Grand Canyon: Rivers of Time_.
    

### **Design & Implementation**

We crafted a high-impact, multi-channel experience combining physical and digital storytelling. The invitation design unfolded beyond initial expectations—mirroring the sense of discovery central to the Grand Canyon itself. Housed in a film tin that resembled an archaeological find, the piece invited recipients to step into history while embracing the future.

On the digital front, a landing page and display ads directed visitors to the Grand Canyon Visitor Center. By integrating modern web design principles with nostalgic storytelling, we ensured both longtime fans and new audiences engaged with the event.
