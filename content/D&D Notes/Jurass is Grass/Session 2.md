## Notes
We're told that Sara is a Princess. She wanted to travel alone and enjoy the countryside before. Her missing means that it could cause a war. Thistlewind the Harengon went north to retrieve her while the party travelled south to try and activate the SOS Beacon.

Raptors attacked and were swiftly defeated. 

Errawr seems to have pulled the beacon to travel away. He was pursued by three terror birds (big freakin Emus) and the party chose to help save him.

Rook broke both of his kneecaps and Goldblum found the beacon. Everyone went back to camp and long rested for the night.
